FID: Frechet INception Distance: Compares teh distribution of generated images to the distribution of real images, measuring how similar they are. 
- Lower FID indicates better quality of generated images. 
- FID is sensitive to mode collapse: If the GAN generates only a few diverse images, FID will be high. 
- Common Baseline: It provides a standardized way to compare GAM performance across studies. 
- FID is best computed using a large number of images 
- Basically, take images, pass through a feature extractor like Inception V3, get low dimensional representation, get their multivariate gaussian distribution, calculate the FID score between these distributions.
